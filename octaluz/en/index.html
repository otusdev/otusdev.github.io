How to play

  • Drag pieces from the tray onto the board.
  • Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clearing several at once scores more.
  • Keep clearing on your next moves to build a combo and multiply your points.
  • A piece won’t fit right now? Put it in the hold slot and use it later.
  • In a tight spot? Use boosters: hammer, bomb, rotate and undo.
  • Clear the whole board for a bonus.
  • The game ends when no piece fits on the board anymore.

Daily Light

Every day brings a new challenge, the same for everyone. Beat the goal to turn on the light and earn a free continue. Days in a row build your streak, which pays out boosters and shields for the days you can’t play. Trophies on the calendar unlock new board themes. Then share your score and challenge your friends.
